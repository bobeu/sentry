## Vision

Sentry is an AI Community Employee that businesses and individuals hire to work inside Telegram communities.

Unlike traditional Telegram bots that only respond to commands, Sentry behaves like a digital employee.

Users employ Sentry by funding a prepaid on-chain wallet.

As Sentry completes work, it deducts small payments from the user's balance.

When funds are exhausted, Sentry automatically stops working until the wallet is funded again.

## Payment Model

Users never pay subscriptions.

Instead:

```
Deposit

↓

Wallet Balance

↓

Agent Works

↓

Charge

↓

Wallet Decreases

↓

Balance Empty

↓

Agent Stops
```

Simple.
